# v2026.10.24-6 pre-release tests

Tested in the afternoon of 2026-10-25. It is
[v2026.10.24-5](v2026.10.24-5.md) with what
[its campaign](v2026.10.24-5.md) found:

- a libvirt host is told by the SSH host key the Controller pins for its
  record, no longer by libvirt's UUID - random on a board without an
  SMBIOS UUID, a placeholder many boards share on others
  ([#292](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/292));
- a version bringing VRRP to an auto-scaled cluster is refused, and the
  engine doesn't act on a cluster managing VRRP
  ([#293](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/293));
- a machine's API view gives the host it runs on, and a host only named
  another way isn't journaled as a move
  ([#294](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/294)).

The same kernel and HAProxy as v2026.10.24-5. The lab gained a **second
KVM host** since: the clusters' anti-affinity is tested on two real
hosts for the first time. The baseline compared with
[v2026.10.24-5](v2026.10.24-5.md)'s figures, then what this pre-release
changes, on the lab's real nodes, its Controller and its two KVM hosts.

## Verdict

**Ready to promote.** The three fixes hold on the lab, and the clusters'
placement does what it says across two real hosts:

- **#292**: after the update, each host's identity is its SSH host key's
  fingerprint - the one `ssh-keyscan` reads from it -, the two hosts
  distinct, a second record reaching the first host sharing its
  identity;
- **#294**: every machine's API view gives its host, and none was
  journaled as moved when the identities changed form;
- **anti-affinity on two hosts**: three machines asked for with a host
  each **refused before anything was made**, naming both hosts (and the
  two records reaching one); two machines placed **one on each host**; a
  member recycled onto the only host left free; a third member refused,
  saying which member runs where;
- **#293**: with auto-scaling observing or on, applying a version that
  manages VRRP was refused (409); with it off, the version went through
  and the VIP answered; auto-scaling then refused on the VRRP cluster;
- a cluster with anti-affinity preferred, scaled 2 → 3 → 2 under load:
  **nothing lost**, no error.

Nothing regressed: the Controller's update, the image factory, the
rolling update under load (**nothing lost on the VIP**), the failovers,
the reboots, the rollback and the throughput are level with
v2026.10.24-5's; zero AVC denials, the CIS audit 33/33.

One firmware reboot of a node took **43.7 s** instead of 9.4 s - the
intermittent slow reboot seen in earlier campaigns on update reboots,
now located between janusd's reboot line and the next kernel; not
reproduced in four timestamped tries ([finding 1](#findings)). Two
cosmetic findings ([findings 2 and 3](#findings)); the journal one is
fixed in [#297](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/297).

## What this pre-release changes

### A host is its SSH host key (#292), and machines show it (#294)

The lab's Controller updated to v2026.10.24-6, its two KVM hosts' records
read again:

| Record | v2026.10.24-5 | v2026.10.24-6 |
|---|---|---|
| The first KVM host | `libvirt:03000200-0400-0500-0006-000700080009` - the "To Be Filled By O.E.M." placeholder | `libvirt:SHA256:KASpY7hk…` = the host's SSH host key as `ssh-keyscan` reads it |
| The second KVM host (no SMBIOS UUID: libvirtd made one up at each start until the host was told to use its machine-id) | `libvirt:60e1be73-…` | `libvirt:SHA256:D+bNE5xm…` = its SSH host key |
| A second record reaching the first host (another address for the same SSH server), added for the test | (scenario 40: the placeholder too) | the first host's identity |

Each record took the new form at its first status read after the
update. The six machines the lab keeps took it too, at their next sync,
and their API views (`GET /api/machines`) gave `host_id` and
`host_name`; none was journaled as moved by the change of form. A
machine just made has no host in its view until its first sync - under
a minute - while its cluster's page already shows the host from its
hypervisor's record.

### Anti-affinity on two real hosts (#283, with #292)

A cluster's machines asked for on three records - the two hosts' and the
second record reaching the first host:

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| Three new machines, anti-affinity **required** | **refused before anything was made** (400): "3 new machine(s) need as many hosts no member runs on - the hypervisors given reach 2 host(s): the first host (its two records: one host); the second host"; no machine, no cluster |
| Two new machines, **required** | **made in 25 s**, one machine on each host (the libvirt domains where the journal said), no problem on the page |
| A member recycled | 17 s: the new machine **placed on the only host no other member used** - the replaced member's -, joined drained, the swap, the old member out and its machine destroyed, the ticket ring renewed |
| Three members asked for (`POST …/scale`) | accepted (202), then **refused in the cluster's error** - "the hypervisors given reach 2 host(s): the first host …, where one member runs; the second host, where the other runs"; no machine made |

### Scaling under load, and VRRP under auto-scaling (#287, #293)

A second cluster, anti-affinity **preferred**, on the two hosts, given a
version with a frontend answering on port 80 and tracking every request
in a stick table; one runner loading each member.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| Members set to 3, then 2, under `wrk` on both members (19,478 + 20,312 req/s) | **3 in 17 s** - the new machine on the first host, the placement journaled ("a machine goes to host …, which already has 1 member(s) of the cluster - anti-affinity preferred, not enough hosts"); **2 in 3 s**; the probes of both members **nothing lost** (3,265 and 3,264); `wrk`: **no error** in 8 million requests |
| Auto-scaling **observe**, then a version with a keepalived template (its own VIP, priorities as members' variables) applied managing VRRP | **refused, 409**: "auto-scaling is observe: a VRRP cluster can't scale (one member holds each virtual IP, a member more adds no capacity) - set it off first"; the members kept the version before |
| The same with auto-scaling **on** | **refused, 409** ("auto-scaling is on: …") |
| Auto-scaling **off**, the version applied again | went through; both members on it, the VIP answered by the member with the higher priority |
| Auto-scaling asked to observe, then on | **refused, 400**: "a VRRP cluster: one member holds each virtual IP, a member more adds no capacity" |

## Findings

1. **One slow firmware reboot, 43.7 s.** The intermittent slow reboot
   earlier campaigns saw on update reboots (57 and 72 s), this time on
   the first plain firmware reboot of node 02 after its update. janusd's
   own line ("rebooting now - haproxy stopped 2.112s, shutdown hooks
   11ms, sync 1ms") came at the usual moment; the next kernel's first
   line came **37 s** later, where 4 s is the rule (the console's drain
   at most 2 s, the reset and the firmware about 2 s). Of the serial
   console's last reboots, this one alone shows two `net front:
   Unexpected TXQ (0) queue failure: -5` lines - virtio-net failing to
   send - between the kernel's "Restarting system" and "machine
   restart". Four more firmware reboots with the console timestamped on
   the host took 9.2 to 9.5 s each (the reset to the firmware's boot
   loader 1.9 to 2.2 s). Not a regression of this pre-release; the
   lead - the kernel's own shutdown, around the network device, rather
   than the firmware - is for the open follow-up.
2. **A refused step out answers 202.** Asking a cluster with
   anti-affinity required for a member more than its hosts allow is
   accepted, then refused in the cluster's error a moment later; a new
   cluster asked for the same is refused at once (400). The reason given
   is clear either way.
3. **The journal named a hypervisor by its ID** when a member was added
   or recycled ("a new machine like …, on a1b579bad3af2c9d").
   [#297](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/297) names it, checked
   by the unit tests and on `controller-libvirt-test`'s journal.
4. **The old member's drain journaled twice** in a recycle - cosmetic,
   as in the previous campaigns.
