# v2026.11.02-2 pre-release tests

Tested on 2026-11-02, the afternoon it came out, straight after
[v2026.11.02](v2026.11.02.md)'s campaign the same morning. It is
v2026.11.02 with what that campaign found fixed
([#476](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/476)):

- **the connection handoff isn't offered while a member's release lacks
  it** - a node now says it has it (`ClusterInfo.handoff_supported`), else
  the choice is greyed out with "*member*: its release has no connection
  handoff - update the cluster without it first"
  ([#475](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/475));
- **a standby made by a switch-over no longer says "standby too" for a
  minute**: a peer's state says its stored role, and a failed copy is
  tried again within 5 s
  ([#477](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/477));
- **`dashboardd promote` asks the active's state first**, 10 s at most,
  instead of waiting a minute for an active out of reach (#477);
- **leaving a cluster ends its connection handoff** (#477);
- the rescue volume's ISO goes on SATA, IDE or virtio-scsi (docs only).

The campaign was short, as the brief asked: the Controller's update, the
image factory, the cluster and #475 (an update in place onto the
pre-release under load, then two updates with the handoff under long
connections, then the cluster's deletion), the warm standby and its
witness again for #477, two nodes' updates and boot stages, an A/B of
HAProxy's CPU cost against v2026.11.02, the nodes' health and the pages.
v2026.11.02's figures are from its own campaign this morning, on the
same lab.

## Verdict

**Every fix of this pre-release does what it says** on the lab, and
the baseline holds. But the campaign found **one new defect, in the warm
standby that v2026.11.02 introduced**: a Controller that has once been a
standby can no longer update itself through its updater
([Findings](#findings), 1). It fails safe - nothing changed, the
Controller started again on its version - and a file deleted by hand
gets it going, but it hits exactly the Controllers that adopt the
standby, after their first switch-over. **My opinion: fix it before
promoting** (the fix looks small); the rest does not stand in the way.
The decision is yours.

- **#475**: with both members on v2026.11.02, the handoff is greyed out
  with the reason for each member - in `upgrade-options`, in the Update
  dialog, refused (400) when asked for anyway, nothing drained;
  `janusctl cluster get` says "unavailable - not in this node's release".
  Updated in place onto v2026.11.02-2 without it (36.3 s under load), the
  handoff became available; two updates with it then kept **every long
  connection: 0 cut out of 419, twice** (v2026.11.02's control update
  without it: 77 cut out of 394).
- **#477, leaving a cluster**: after the deletion, no member shows a
  handoff any more, the copies' readings stopped, the live ruleset is
  empty, every configuration is as before the cluster.
- **#477, the switch-over**: three switch-overs (1.8, 1.8 and 1.9 s), and
  on each new standby, read every 5 s for 70 s, **not one "standby too"
  line**; its first copy came 5 s after the switch-over.
- **#477, the promotion behind a partition**: `promote` refused in
  **10.4 s** (v2026.11.02: 60 s), `promote -force` done in **10.8 s**
  (60 s); the old active stepped down through the Consul witness 5.1 s
  after the promotion wrote it, **with no moment where both answered as
  the active** (v2026.11.02: 11.9 s).
- The nodes saw nothing of the Controllers' moves; 0 AVC denials, no
  OOM kill, no oops, enforcing, CIS 33/33 on the four nodes updated; the
  pages axe-clean in two palettes and both themes.

## What this pre-release changes

campaign before - the configurations before it identical to that
campaign's:

  its release has no connection handoff - update the cluster without it
  first; node-02: its release has no connection handoff - update the

- `janusctl cluster get` (v2026.11.02-2's janusctl) on node 01:
  "Handoff: unavailable - not in this node's release".

## Findings

1. **A Controller that has been a standby can't update itself.** The
   lab Controller's update to this pre-release failed at the updater's
   backup: "Backing up failed, nothing was changed: /data/ha/control.sock:
   not a regular file, directory or symlink". A standby listens on
   `ha/control.sock` (for `dashboardd promote`) and nothing removes it
   when the Controller becomes the active again - this one was left by
   v2026.11.02's campaign, where the lab Controller was a standby for
   a few minutes; and the updater's copy of the data directory refuses
   anything but files, directories and links. Every switch-over leaves
   one: after this campaign's, the lab Controller had a new one. The
   updater did the right thing - it stopped the Controller, failed the
   backup, started the same version again (2.6 s without an answer),
   nothing changed - and deleting the socket by hand let the update
   through. It is in v2026.11.02 too (both files unchanged since), but
   only a Controller that has used the warm standby has the socket. The
   fix could be either side: the standby removing its socket when it
   stops being one (or the active removing a stale one at its start),
   and the updater's copy skipping sockets.
2. **`ha/previous/` makes every update longer.** Each step-down sets the
   whole data directory aside there, and nothing prunes it: the lab
   Controller had 3 after the campaign before (186 of its 237 files),
   5 after this one. The updater copies and syncs every file while the
   Controller is stopped: the backup took 12 s, the update
   **14.6 s without an answer** instead of 4.6 s. Pruning to the last
   one or two would keep the rollback this exists for.
3. **Right after a switch-over, the new standby shows an error for 5 s**:
   its first copy is refused with "503 ... this Controller is a standby:
   the active answers" (the promoted one is starting again), shown by `ha
   status` and `/api/ha` until the retry 5 s later; and in that first
   second `ha status` showed the copy left from an earlier time the
   Controller was a standby ("3h36m ago, epoch 5"). Cosmetic - much
   better than v2026.11.02's minute of "standby too".

Also: the upgrade call answering in 8.1 s and the A/B's few percent
(above) - observations, not defects as far as I can tell. Not this
release: the lab's log profile's Logstash target is out of the lab's
reach (`failing`), as before.
