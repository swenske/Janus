# v2026.10.29-3 pre-release tests

Tested at midday on 2026-10-29: a short pass on what changed since
[v2026.10.29-2](v2026.10.29-2.md), whose campaign found the defects this
pre-release fixes. It is v2026.10.29-2 with
[#406](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/406) (a stopped HAProxy puts
a node's services in maintenance even when it can't read its maps; the
Prometheus example drops a drained node's URLs) and
[#408](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/408) (`PATCH
/api/hypervisors/{id}` keeps the fields it leaves out; a node on an
earlier release the Consul selector doesn't pick reads "not picked"; the
docs' screenshots with the Consul tab). Everything not run again here -
the failovers, the reboots, the rollback, the throughput, the labels and
most of the Consul scenario - is in [v2026.10.29-2's
report](v2026.10.29-2.md).

## Verdict

**Ready to promote.** Both fixes do what they say on the lab's nodes:

- **HAProxy stopped**, on a node whose configuration reads a map, puts
  every one of its services in maintenance - its URL instances
  included - about a second later, and takes them out about a second
  after HAProxy starts; v2026.10.29-2 left them out of maintenance.
- **The Prometheus example**, as published, stops probing the URLs of
  drained members, and keeps probing the members still in service.
- **A hypervisor's labels** change with `labels` alone, the rest of the
  record kept; a node the selector doesn't pick reads "not picked", and
  no longer brings the Controller's Consul pass back every minute.

Nothing new was found.

## The lab

Consul server next to the lab's Controller (ACLs denying by default,
gossip encryption, RPC TLS both ways, `auto_encrypt`, HTTPS only), the

hypervisors labelled, machines A and B (a cluster, its configuration
