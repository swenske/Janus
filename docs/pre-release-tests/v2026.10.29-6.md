# v2026.10.29-6 pre-release tests

Tested on the evening of 2026-10-29: a short pass on what changed since
[v2026.10.29-5](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/421) - the
Controller asking the image factory's mirror when the factory can't be
reached ([#417](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/417)) and the
opt-in usage report ([#419](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/419)).
The rest of what it brings runs on the image factory's site, deployed
when it merged: the mirror itself
([#420](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/420)) and the builder's
download counts ([#416](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/416)).
What isn't run again here is in the reports of
[v2026.10.29-3](v2026.10.29-3.md), [v2026.10.29-4](v2026.10.29-4.md)
and v2026.10.29-5's.

## Verdict

**Ready to promote.** What it brings works with the lab's Controller,
the production mirror and the production receiver:

- **A machine updates through the mirror**: with the image factory
  unreachable, the Controller found the update on the mirror and the
  node downloaded its bundle from it - 10.3 s from the change to the
  node running the release, healthy.
- **The node page's update check says where the update comes from**:
  "the image factory's mirror", ready, in 4.9 s.
- **The usage report is off until turned on**, then sent at once, kept
  by the receiver without anything about the lab; **New ID** made the
  receiver forget the previous one; turned off, nothing is due and
  **Send now** is refused.
- **The Controller's own update** took as long as v2026.10.29-5's.

No defect found.

### Seen in production meanwhile

The monitoring probes every URL the nodes list in Consul (blackbox,
`examples/prometheus/consul.yml`'s job). The mirror's and the
receiver's hosts are APIs: their root answered 404, so their probes
failed. Their front ends now take the root to the service's health
check - the probes test the services themselves. A configuration
matter, not the pre-release's.
