# v2026.10.20-3 pre-release tests

Tested on the night of 2026-10-20 and the morning after. It is
[v2026.10.20-2](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/releases/tag/v2026.10.20-2)
with the console plan's two options, after the question of init's CPU
on a production node:

- the services' share of the console, settable per node - down to
  nothing ([#202](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/202));
- a schematic without the serial console, `"console": "screen"`
  ([#203](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/203)).

HAProxy, the kernels and the extensions are v2026.10.19-4's still. The
lab ran v2026.10.20-2, measured again that night before the update. This
run repeats every step of [v2026.10.20-2](v2026.10.20-2.md#the-lab)'s
campaign and adds the two options on real nodes. The scenarios:
[Lab test scenarios](lab-scenarios.md).

## Verdict

**Not ready to promote as is.** The console's share works on real
nodes, each way it shows; the throughput, the failovers, the reboots,
the rollbacks and the nodes' health are the release's. Two defects
around the screen-only schematic, both fixed on `main` after this
build:

- its `schematic-catalog.json` lists no console choice, so the image
  factory refuses the screen-only schematic for this release
  ([#205](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/205));
- on a screen-only node, init still mirrored the console to the
  screen: **every line showed twice**
  ([#206](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/206)).

The next pre-release carries both; a short check of a factory-built
screen-only machine is what it needs before a promotion.

### The console's share, under load

Node 02, HAProxy logging every request to its stdout under `wrk` from
a runner (two threads, 64 keep-alive connections, about 33,000
requests/s), init's and janusd's CPU over 60 s (`x-budget`):

| The share | HAProxy | init | janusd |
|---|---|---|---|
| the default, 2 KiB/s | 32,169 req/s | **2.46 %** | 19.0 % |
| **0**, set with `janusctl system console-budget -rate 0` | 35,099 req/s | **0.05 %** | 19.6 % |
| the default again, `-default` | 33,135 req/s | **3.46 %** | 19.2 % |

- **init's CPU is the console's share**: nothing of it left at 0, the
  same again at the default, under the same load; janusd's cost per
  logged line - the ring, the LOGS partition, the shipping - is
  untouched by the setting, as expected. HAProxy served 9 % more with
  the share at 0 (one run each: a trend).
- **Each way the setting shows agrees**: `janusctl` ("none of the
  services' output"), the Controller's relay (`rate_bytes_per_second`
  absent, `burst_bytes` 65536), the exporter
  (`janus_console_budget_bytes_per_second 0`,
  `janus_console_lines_left_out_total{service="haproxy"}` 14.7 million
  since janusd started - every line still in `system logs haproxy`).
- The node's own log, a setting's event and the notes about lines left
  out still reach the console at 0: janusd's own output is never
  limited.
- The first attempt of this step measured nothing at 0: the lab's
  `janusctl` was still v2026.10.20's, without the command. Refreshed
  from the release's `.deb` (sha256 checked against the asset's digest),
  as every campaign should.

### A node without a serial console

A node made by hand on the lab's host from the screen-only image
(`{"customization":{"console":"screen"}}`, built on the build host
from `main` - the factory refuses it for this release, above), with
the lab's usual NoCloud provisioning: static addresses, the lab
Controller (`screen-machine`):

- **Nothing on its serial port**: its serial log holds 266 bytes, the
  firmware's two `BdsDxe` lines - no kernel, no init, no first-boot
  credentials. The lab's `janus-creds`, which reads them there, saved
  none: a screen-only node is read through the Controller alone.
- **Registered on its own**: announced to the Controller at its first
  boot, approved from the pending list, trusted its fleet 60 s later,
  its log forwarding profile applied at admission.
- **Its screen** (the host's `virsh screenshot`): the first boot's
  credentials, the motd and janusd's log - **each line twice** with the
  release's init (the mirror to `/dev/tty0`, whose `/dev/console` this
  already is); **once** with [#206](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/206)'s,
  the image rebuilt with it and the machine made again.
- **Its health**: v2026.10.20-3 (the build host's `main`), kernel
  7.2.10, `/usr/lib/janus/image.json` naming the schematic
  `{"customization":{"console":"screen"}}`, SELinux enforcing, **0** AVC
  denials, no oops, CIS **33/33**; the console's share at its default.
- **Under load** (`x-screen-load`, through the Controller's relay: the
  bootstrap configuration made to log every request to stdout, `wrk`
  from a runner on its `:8080` for 60 s):

| | HAProxy | init | janusd |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle | - | 0.02 % | 0.03 % |
| logging every request, the share at its default | 65,686 req/s | **0.02 %** | 28.8 % |

  The share still flows - 4.8 million lines left out in that minute,
  the rest on the screen - but the screen costs init nothing where the
  emulated serial port cost it 2.5 % on node 02 above. The request
  rate isn't node 02's: a bootstrap configuration answering itself,
  not the lab's.
