# v2026.10.24-5 pre-release tests

Tested late in the morning of 2026-10-25. It is
[v2026.10.24-4](v2026.10.24-4.md) with what
[its campaign](v2026.10.24-4.md) found, and two new phases of the
clusters:

- a janusd restart, and a shutdown by the hypervisor through the guest
  agent, drain the node first: the virtual IP goes to the peer before
  keepalived stops, keepalived and BIRD stop last
  ([#285](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/285));
- `janusctl`'s `-h` shows a command's help and runs nothing
  ([#284](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/284));
- **clusters made on hypervisors**: the Controller makes a cluster's
  machines itself, each on a physical host of its own (anti-affinity),
  telling which host a hypervisor record reaches
  ([#283](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/283));
- **horizontal auto-scaling**: such a cluster's members set by hand,
  or by an engine that adds one under load and takes one out when quiet
  ([#287](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/287), with a fix to two
  machines sharing a base image upload);
- two test fixes ([#289](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/289),
  [#290](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/290)).

The same kernel and HAProxy as v2026.10.24-4. The baseline compared with
[v2026.10.24-4](v2026.10.24-4.md)'s figures, then what this pre-release
changes, on the lab's real nodes, its Controller and its KVM host.

## Verdict

**Not ready as is**, for two findings in the new cluster features, both
with their fixes:

1. **A libvirt host is told by its SMBIOS UUID alone**, and many boards
   carry their maker's placeholder one - **the lab's own host does**
   (`03000200-0400-0500-0006-000700080009`, "To Be Filled By O.E.M.").
   Two such hosts would count as one: a cluster asking for a host of
   its own per member refused, the page warning of members on one host
   when they aren't. Never two members placed together unseen - the
   error is on the safe side, but it stops the feature on such hardware
   ([finding 1](#findings), [#292](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/292)).
2. **Auto-scaling's VRRP refusal can be walked around**: turning it on
   for a VRRP cluster is refused, but with it on, a version that brings
   VRRP in was applied and auto-scaling stayed on over a VRRP cluster
   ([finding 2](#findings), [#293](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/293)).

The machine's API view doesn't show the host it runs on either
([finding 3](#findings), [#294](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/294)).

Everything else does what it says. **#285 holds on the lab**: the VRRP
master's janusd restarted under load - by `janusctl` and from its node
page - and the master shut down by its hypervisor through the guest
agent **lost nothing on the virtual IP** (v2026.10.24-4: 3 and 2), the
peer taking it over by priority; a node alone is without its IP 3.0 s
instead of 4.9 s. **#284 holds**: `-h` on restart, shutdown and reboot
printed their help, janusd untouched. **The new features work end to
end on the lab's KVM host**: a cluster refused before anything was made
when the hosts don't allow it, made on one host with anti-affinity
preferred and said so, a member recycled and placed, the members scaled
2 → 3 → 2 under load with no error, and **auto-scaling adding a member
under the runners' load and taking it out once quiet**, from the real
load, journaling why. Nothing regressed: the Controller's update, the
image factory, the rolling update under load (nothing lost on the VIP),
the failovers, the reboots and the throughput are level with
v2026.10.24-4's.

A next pre-release with #292, #293 and #294.

## What this pre-release changes

### A janusd restart hands the virtual IP over (#285)

`janusd` restarted, and the node shut down by its hypervisor, with
`wrk` on the VIP (8 keep-alive connections) and the probes every 50 ms
on the VIP and the anycast address; the extension daemons listed before
and after.

| What | v2026.10.24-4 | v2026.10.24-5 |
|---|---|---|
| Node 01, the VRRP master, `janusctl system restart` | **VIP: 3 lost** at the swap; anycast nothing | **VIP: nothing lost at the swaps** (node 02 holding it 4.3 s; two lost 11 s and 30 s later, one of them on both addresses at once - the lab's path); anycast: 2 lost at the swap, as at every drained swap |
| Node 01, from its node page (Restart janusd) | (node 02, the backup, nothing lost) | **VIP: nothing lost** (node 02 holding it 4.2 s); anycast: 1 at the swap back |
| Node 01 shut down by its hypervisor through the guest agent | **VIP: 2 lost** at the swap | **VIP: nothing lost** of 1,379; anycast: 1 at the swap back; off in 3.9 s |
| A machine alone (every extension, a VRRP instance of its own, no peer) | its virtual IP gone **4.94 s** | gone **2.99 s**; its own address nothing lost |

Each time the daemons came back one of each (keepalived's two
processes), every service healthy, zero AVC denials; the API answered
again 1 s after the restart's call. On the machine alone (janusd's lines
from the lab's Elasticsearch): the drain 2 s after the call - with no
peer, the virtual IP stays where it is and HAProxy serves on -, the
other extensions stopped 2 s later, Consul's leave 3 s, keepalived
stopped last (the IP gone at that moment), init's 1 s before the next
janusd, which started keepalived about 1 s later; the IP was back 0.8 s
after that. A first try on that machine measured 3.86 s: its janusd had
run 50 s only, and init doubles its restart delay for a process that
ran under a minute.

### `-h` runs nothing (#284)

On the every-extension machine with the pre-release's `janusctl`:
`system restart -h`, `system shutdown --help` and `system reboot -help`
printed their usage; janusd's process the same before and after, the
node up.

### Clusters made on hypervisors (#283)

On the lab's Controller and its one KVM host:

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| A cluster of two new machines, anti-affinity **required**, the KVM host's record | **refused before anything was made**: "2 new machine(s) need as many hosts no member runs on - the hypervisors given reach 1 host(s)"; no machine, no cluster |
| A second hypervisor record reaching the same host (another address for the same SSH server) | trusted; **the same host identity** as the first record |
| Two new machines, anti-affinity **preferred**, both records | **made in 22 s** (the machines ready in 10 s), both on the host; the journal said a machine went to a host that already had a member; the page: "plc-1 and plc-2 run on the same host … losing it stops them all", each member's host under its node, the placement in the header |
| A member recycled | 18 s: the new machine placed (the same warning journaled), joined drained, the swap, the old member out, its machine destroyed, the ticket ring renewed |

The pages these features add, in headless Chromium - the cluster's page
(its Placement, the hosts, the Scaling card) and the New cluster form's
**New machines** (both records listed with their host, the networks they
share offered) - in two palettes and both modes: 20 pages, no page
error, no console error, no failed request, no axe finding.

### Horizontal auto-scaling (#287)

The cluster above, given a version with a frontend answering on port
80 and tracking every request in a stick table. The clients sent to the
members directly - one runner to each, as a balancer in front would.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| Members set to 3, then 2, under `wrk` on both members (18,918 + 18,812 req/s) | **3 in 19 s** - a new machine like the newest member, placed, joined; **2 in 1 s** - the newest drained, out, its machine destroyed; **`wrk`: no error** in 7.5 million requests; the probes: 2 lost of 3,288 on one member, at moments nothing happened there (the lab's path), none on the other |
| Auto-scaling **observe**, the runners loading the members (`wrk` 4 threads, 256 connections each; one vCPU per member) | the load read: HAProxy idle 1.5 %, the CPUs 99 % busy; journaled "would add a member (2 → 3): over 1m0s with 2 members: HAProxy idle 26 % < 30 %, CPUs 98 % busy > 75 % - observing"; the count simulated, nothing made |
| Auto-scaling **on**, under the same load | 49 s later "adding a member (2 → 3)"; the machine made and joined, 71 s from the policy; `wrk` no error (47,945 + 49,631 req/s) |
| The load stopped | **301 s later** - the policy's 5 minutes - "taking a member out (3 → 2): throughout 5m0s, 2 members would still see HAProxy idle 50 % or more…"; the newest drained, out, destroyed |
| A VRRP cluster (a version with a keepalived template, its VIP answering) | turning auto-scaling on or to observe **refused**: "a VRRP cluster: one member holds each virtual IP, a member more adds no capacity" |

The policy's windows were set to their shortest (a minute out, five
minutes in) to fit the campaign. What the step out showed too: with the
clients sending to two members, **the member added took no traffic**,
and those two stayed as busy - auto-scaling adds capacity only where
the traffic follows the members, BGP/ECMP or a balancer that learns
them, as [the design](../cluster.md#6-horizontal-auto-scaling) says;
the balancer's hook is still to come.

## Findings

1. **A libvirt host told by its SMBIOS UUID alone.** The Controller
   names a libvirt host `libvirt:<UUID>`, the UUID from libvirt's
   capabilities (SMBIOS). The lab's KVM host reports
   `03000200-0400-0500-0006-000700080009`: the placeholder many boards
   leave ("To Be Filled By O.E.M."). Two hosts with it count as one -
   a cluster asking for a host of its own per member refused, members on
   two such hosts reported together. Nor is the UUID steady: a host's
   libvirtd may report a new one at each start.
   [#292](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/292): a libvirt host is
   told by the SSH host key pinned for its record - two records reaching
   one host still share it, two hosts never do.
2. **Bringing VRRP in under auto-scaling.** Turning auto-scaling on or
   to observe is refused on a VRRP cluster; but with it on, applying a
   version that manages VRRP went through, and auto-scaling stayed on
   over a VRRP cluster, nothing said.
   [#293](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/293): that apply is
   refused while auto-scaling isn't off ("set it off first"), and the
   engine never steps on a cluster managing VRRP - it says so in its
   status and journal (a cluster made before the refusal).
   `controller-libvirt-test` applies such a version under observing
   auto-scaling: 409.
3. **A machine's host not in its API view.** The sync records the host a
   machine runs on (every lab machine had it), the cluster's page shows
   it, the release notes and the hypervisors page say machines show it,
   but `GET /api/machines/{id}` didn't.
   [#294](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/294): the view carries
   `host_id` and `host_name`, checked on `controller-libvirt-test`'s
   machines; and a machine whose host is only named another way (#292's
   new identities) isn't journaled as moved.
4. **The old member's drain journaled twice** in a recycle - cosmetic,
   as in the previous campaigns.

## Not tested

- **Proxmox VE**: the node identity and a VM's node read from the
  cluster's resources need a live PVE; the lab has none.
- **A cluster across several hosts**: the lab has one KVM host - the
  placement on distinct hosts is proven by the unit tests and the
  refusal on the lab.
- **Auto-scaling with BGP/ECMP or a balancer following the members**:
  the lab's anycast is the two nodes', not a cluster made on the
  hypervisor; the step out was proven with the clients sending to the
  members directly.
- **A janusd killed hard**, **`janusctl cluster` from a fleet context**,
  **restoring a backup**, **arm64**, **IPv6**: as in
  [v2026.10.24-4](v2026.10.24-4.md#not-tested).
