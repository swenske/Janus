# v2026.10.29-2 pre-release tests

Tested on the morning of 2026-10-29. It is
[v2026.10.29](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/releases/tag/v2026.10.29)
with [#400](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/400) (the Controller
manages the nodes' Consul agents: each node's own ACL token, a managed
layer of its agent's configuration, and the node registering janusd, its
exporters, its HAProxy frontends and the URLs they route) and
[#401](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/401) (hypervisors have
labels, and the nodes of their machines inherit them - grants, scoped
tokens, single sign-on, log forwarding and Consul's node metadata use
them).

## Verdict

**Not ready as is** - two defects in #400, both on what a node does
while it doesn't serve; [#406](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/406)
fixes both. Everything else is ready:

- **The baseline holds**: the Controller's update, the cluster updated
  in place under load without losing a request, failovers, firmware and
  kexec reboots and the rollback at the level of v2026.10.29's
  campaign; zero AVC denials and the CIS audit 33/33 on every node.
- **Hypervisors' labels** reach the nodes of their machines at once -
  the node's own label winning -, and a scoped token follows them both
  ways, through the API and janusctl.
- **Consul through the Controller** works end to end against a Consul
  set up as production asks (ACLs denying by default, gossip
  encryption, RPC TLS verified both ways, `auto_encrypt`, HTTPS only):
  the Test, each node's token, layer and metadata, every service where
  it really is, a runtime map change in Consul within a second, drain
  and undrain, a reboot, a node taken out, removed or the integration
  turned off - and Prometheus finding the exporters and probing the
  URLs from it.

The defects ([findings 1 and 2](#findings)):

1. **A node whose HAProxy is stopped doesn't put its services in
   maintenance when its configuration reads a map** - the release
   notes and the guide say it does; its URL instances, which have no
   check of their own, stay passing for as long as HAProxy is stopped.
2. **The Prometheus example's drop rule doesn't drop a drained node's
   URLs**: Prometheus reads a service in maintenance as `maintenance`,
   the rule matches `critical` only.

## Findings

1. **HAProxy stopped, a node whose configuration reads a map keeps its
   services out of maintenance.** The node's registration reads the
   maps from the running HAProxy, through its stats socket: with HAProxy
   stopped that read fails - "consul: the node's services: map
   …/hosts.map: dial stats socket: … connection refused - trying again"
   in janusd's log - before the services are put in maintenance. The
   frontends' services went critical by their own TCP check about 6 s
   later; the URL instances, which have no check, stayed passing until
   HAProxy was started again. A node without a map (C) went into
   maintenance 0.85 s after the stop; drain, reboots and shutdowns are
   not concerned. Fixed in
   [#406](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/406): when the node is
   out of service and what it serves can't be read, the services
   already registered go in maintenance all the same. A unit test and a
   new step of `qemu-consul-controller-test` (the test's node A reads a
   map) both fail without it and pass with it, on the build box.
2. **The example's drop rule misses a drained node's URLs.** Prometheus's
   Consul discovery gives a service with a maintenance check
   `__meta_consul_health="maintenance"`, not `critical`, so
   `regex: critical` never drops it: with both members drained, the
   three targets were still probed. `critical|maintenance` does what the
   guide says. Fixed in the example and the guide in #406, which also
   says that the discovery needs `tls_config` with Consul's own CA.
3. **A node the selector doesn't pick, on an older release, reads
   "unsupported - update it"** in the Controller's Consul table (the
   screen-only node, no labels, while the selector was `dc=lab`): the
   release is checked before the selector. Cosmetic; the table's member
   column also lags one pass after a node leaves or joins.
4. **After its token was made again**, a node's services restored by
   its agent with the deleted token were refused by the servers ("ACL
   not found"); janusd registered all ten again with the new token at
   once, but only the three whose check changed state reached the
   catalog until the agent's own full sync, 61 s later. Consul's
   behaviour; noted.
5. **The docs' screenshots weren't refreshed with the Consul tab**:
   image-build's screenshot job (report only) found eight of the
   Controller's pages 0.04 % different - the tab bar's new entry. Not
   in #406; `make docs-screenshots` with the next change to the
   Controller's pages.
6. **`PATCH /api/hypervisors/{id}` replaces the record**: `labels` alone
   is refused ("a name is required"); the page and Terraform send the
   whole record, docs/hypervisors.md describes it as a change. A
   `curl` with labels alone doesn't work.
7. **A cluster version can't bring a map and the configuration that
   reads it at once**: the version is checked on the first member
   before its files are written - "failed to open pattern file
   …/hosts.map". The map first, then the configuration, in two versions,
   works. Cluster engine, not this pre-release.
