# v2026.10.12 pre-release tests

Tested on 2026-10-12, the day it was cut: the CI run that built it, then
the lab, with v2026.10.11-2 (the newest release) measured the same way
just before.

## Verdict

**No regression found.** Every CI test passed. In the lab, the
pre-release updated the Controller and both nodes without losing a
request on the VIP. It failed over as fast as v2026.10.11-2 did and served
the same throughput at the same latency. It rebooted through kexec 5 times
in a row on its patched kernel, and ran SELinux enforcing with no denial
and the CIS benchmark at 33/33.
Its own features worked as intended: the pre-release stayed apart from
the updates everywhere, and the splash screens showed and left.

Found on the way, none of them in the pre-release itself:

- A CI test failed on a false negative, fixed by
  [#47](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/47) - see
  [the CI run](#ci-image-build).
- The Controller answered 5 isolated requests in more than a second
  during the minute after its update - see
  [the Controller](#the-controller).
- The firmware case documented as breaking kexec reboots didn't break
  them on the lab's host, with either release - see [reboots](#reboots).

What the pre-release brings: kernels 7.2.10 (with Janus's patch for
kexec reboots) and 6.18.56, keepalived 2.4.3, the splash screens of
`janusctl tui` and of the Controller, gRPC 1.84.0 -
[its notes](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/releases/tag/v2026.10.12).

## The lab

| Role | What |
|---|---|
| KVM host | Xeon E3-1220 v3 (4 cores), 16 GB, Debian 13, QEMU q35, Debian's OVMF 2025.02 |
| Two Janus nodes | 2 vCPU (host CPU), 1 GiB, OVMF without Secure Boot; a schematic with the five extensions; management, front and backend networks |
| High availability | a VIP by VRRP (VRRPv3, 0.2 s adverts, HAProxy's health weighs -100) and an anycast /32 by BGP to the lab's router (RouterOS 7, BFD 200 ms × 3, no BGP ECMP: one node at a time) |
| Behind | two nginx backends |
| In front | a load client (2 vCPU): `wrk`, and probes - a request every 50 ms on the VIP and on the anycast address, each with a 1 s timeout, so an outage is measured to 50 ms |
| Controller | Docker Compose with its updater, an admin with TOTP |

Each test that changes the nodes ran on v2026.10.11-2 first: the nodes
were updated from v2026.10.07-2 to it the same way the pre-release went
on afterwards.

### The Controller

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| v2026.10.07-3 → v2026.10.11-2, by its updater | updated in 12 s (pull, stop, start, confirmed 10 s later) |
| What v2026.10.11-2 offers | latest v2026.10.11-2, no update available, pre-release v2026.10.12 offered apart |
| Installing v2026.10.12 without confirming it's a pre-release | refused: 409, "v2026.10.12 is a pre-release, for testing - confirm it is the one to install" |
| v2026.10.11-2 → v2026.10.12, confirmed | updated in 12 s; the Controller answered nothing for **1.46 s** (a probe every 0.1 s) |
| Its pages, after | nodes on v2026.10.11-2 show no update (a pre-release isn't one); older nodes are offered v2026.10.11-2 |
| The splash, main page and a node's page | shown at once, gone after 1.4 s (its 1 s minimum, then its fade: the data was already there), both themes, no console error |

The Controller older than v2026.10.11-2 had to be updated first: it
would have taken the pre-release for the newest release.

Observation: during the minute after the update, 5 isolated requests out
of about 650 took more than a second (one every 13 s or so). A plain
restart of the same version showed it once (1.04 s), and at steady state
the same probe saw a median of 6 ms, a 99th percentile of 26 ms and a
maximum of 47 ms. Not a regression - the Controller's backend didn't
change in this pre-release - but worth a look.

### The image factory

The node's page offered v2026.10.12 apart, to prepare. Asked to build
it for the lab's schematic, the factory did so in **61 s**
([schematic-build 38284009120](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/actions/runs/38284009120)),

### Rolling update under load

Both nodes updated one after the other through the Controller (each
node's Upgrade with `wait_for_health`), drained first: its VRRP priority
lowered and its anycast route withdrawn, both restored once it's back in
service. `wrk -t1 -c8` loads the VIP throughout.

| | v2026.10.07-2 → v2026.10.11-2 | v2026.10.11-2 → v2026.10.12 |
|---|---|---|
| VIP | **0** lost / 2,722 | **0** lost / 2,724 |
| Anycast | 5 lost (0.16 s and 0.11 s, one per route switch) | 1 lost (0.06 s) |
| Each node, from Upgrade to back in service | 13.3 s and 14.3 s | 13.3 s and 14.2 s |
| `wrk`, 2.5 min | 7,738 req/s, 99th percentile 4.02 ms | 7,764 req/s, 99th percentile 3.91 ms |

`wrk`'s 16 read errors, in both runs, are its keep-alive connections cut
when the VIP moves - a client reconnects. The anycast address loses what
is in flight when its route moves: RouterOS has no BGP ECMP.

### Failovers

On the node holding the VIP and the anycast route, 60 s of probes each.

| | v2026.10.11-2 | v2026.10.12 (keepalived 2.4.3) |
|---|---|---|
| The node killed (`virsh destroy`), started again 20 s later: VIP | 0.70 s (13 lost) | 0.69 s (13 lost) |
| Same: anycast | 0.59 s (11 lost), then 1 when the route comes back | 0.48 s (9 lost), then 2 when the route comes back |
| HAProxy stopped 10 s, then started: VIP | 0 lost / 1,112 | 0 lost / 1,111 |
| Same: anycast | 2 lost | 0 lost / 1,111 |

A killed node is noticed by VRRP's timer and by BFD: the same 0.5 to
0.7 s with both releases. A stopped HAProxy gives the VIP and the route
away before anything is lost.

### Reboots

On node 02 (the backup), timed from the Reboot call to the API
answering again. Most of it - about 6 s - is the node going down
cleanly: HAProxy drained for 2 s, then soft-stopped.

| | v2026.10.11-2 (7.2.9) | v2026.10.12 (7.2.10, patched) |
|---|---|---|
| Through the firmware | 8.8 s, 9.3 s | 8.7 s, 9.3 s |
| Through kexec, chained | 7.3 s × 3 | 7.3 to 7.4 s × 5 |
| Kernel oops afterwards | none | none |
| Boot stages (Janus exporter) | API 2.15 to 2.17 s after the kernel started | API 2.15 to 2.22 s |

The lab's plain OVMF never showed the bug the patch fixes - 7.2.9 kexecs
cleanly there too - so these runs show no regression, not the fix (CI
shows the fix). One more case was tried: the flash locked to SMM
(libvirt's `secure='yes'`, Secure Boot without enrolled keys) under the
same OVMF 2025.02 - the case documented as making the kexec'd kernel
oops. On this host it didn't, with either release: v2026.10.11-2
kexec'd 3 times and v2026.10.12 5 times, with no oops on the serial
console. Whether that case was the same stale INITRD table as the one
v2026.10.12 fixes (a corruption that depends on where things land in
memory) stays open. The node went back to its usual firmware afterwards.

### Rollback

Node 02 rolled back from v2026.10.12 to v2026.10.11-2 (`janusctl
lifecycle rollback`, slot B to A): API back **8.9 s** after the call, on
v2026.10.11-2 and 7.2.9. A second rollback brought it back to
v2026.10.12.

### HAProxy's throughput

`wrk` from the load client to the VIP: keep-alive with 8, 64 and 256
connections, then a new connection for every request; 30 s each, two
runs (both shown).

| | v2026.10.11-2 | v2026.10.12 |
|---|---|---|
| Keep-alive, 8 connections | 11,236 and 11,183 req/s; 99th percentile 1.39 and 1.44 ms | 11,284 and 11,348 req/s; 1.40 and 1.38 ms |
| Keep-alive, 64 connections | 30,351 and 29,205 req/s; 4.02 and 4.25 ms | 29,655 and 28,844 req/s; 4.23 and 4.36 ms |
| Keep-alive, 256 connections | 34,754 and 33,749 req/s; 13.87 and 14.01 ms | 34,293 and 35,150 req/s; 13.88 and 13.53 ms |
| A new connection per request, 32 connections | 6,966 and 7,195 req/s; 8.23 and 6.42 ms | 7,114 and 7,132 req/s; 6.80 and 6.78 ms |

The same within the runs' spread (3 to 4 %). At 256 connections the
node is the limit: its CPU at 91 % (HAProxy 18 % idle), the client's at
54 %. These are a 2 vCPU VM's figures, to compare releases with each
other - not a capacity measure.

### The nodes' health, on v2026.10.12

Both nodes:

- v2026.10.12, kernel 7.2.10 (stable track), HAProxy 3.4.6, the lab's
  schematic.
- Services: janusd, haproxy, bird, keepalived (`Starting Keepalived
  v2.4.3`), prometheus-node-exporter, qemu-guest-agent - all running
  and healthy.
- SELinux enforcing, **0** AVC denials.
- CIS Debian Linux 13 Benchmark v1.0.0 (Level 2 - Server): **33/33**
  controls compliant.

### janusctl

The pre-release's `.deb`, on a terminal (a pty), against a lab node:
`janusctl tui` opened on its splash at once, its progress from `0/18`
up, and showed the dashboard after 1.01 s.
