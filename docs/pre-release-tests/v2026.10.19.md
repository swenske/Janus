# v2026.10.19 pre-release tests

Tested in the night of 2026-10-18 to 19, right after it was cut. It
brings the Controller's new look
([#167](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/167)): eighteen palettes,
each light and dark, charts that move, segmented gauges, the fleet's
curves on the node list (a new route, `GET /api/nodes/metrics`), HAProxy's
counters broken down on Metrics, particles on the Traffic flow. Nothing
changed on the nodes: their images are v2026.10.18-3's sources, rebuilt.

So the campaign is short for the nodes - the update steps under load,
their health - and long for the Controller's pages: every palette in
both modes, over the lab's seven nodes, with axe, and what an open page
costs a node now that it samples every second. The failovers, reboots,
rollbacks and throughput of [v2026.10.18-2's campaign](v2026.10.18-2.md)
stand for this one. The scenarios: [Lab test scenarios](lab-scenarios.md).

## Verdict

**Ready to promote once [#168](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/168)
is in**, as a v2026.10.19-2: two small defects of the new pages, both
fixed there and checked on the lab's Controller.

- **A status pill on a lighter strip fell under WCAG AA**: Solarized
  dark's "VM running" pill on a machine's strip of the node list, 4.22:1.
  Worked out for every palette, a pill on that strip was under 4.5:1
  for at least one status in 18 of the 36 palettes and modes - the
  release notes say every palette holds AA. The
  default palette had one such pair before this release too (dark, a
  danger pill: 4.23:1, 4.36:1 now). On the lab, axe found it in 7 of
  the 36 palettes and modes; with #168's stylesheet laid over the same
  pages, in none.
- **A title's small capitals took a version with them**: "THE CONTROLLER
  WAS UPDATED TO V2026.10.19", in the notice the lab's Controller showed
  right after its update.
- **The rest holds on real nodes**: no page error, no failed request,
  one console error that came from the browser's machine (below), the curves of all seven nodes, the breakdowns on a real
  HAProxy, the flow's particles. An open node page costs its node's
  janusd **0.28 %** of a CPU at a sample a second, 0.12 % at the old
  five seconds.
- **The nodes**: the update steps match v2026.10.18-3's; 0 AVC denials,
  CIS 33/33.

## The lab

The lab ran v2026.10.18-3, as its campaign left it. The load and the
probes came from the runners, as in the last campaigns, so that the
figures compare; the fourth client machine, back with its memory
replaced, ran the browser. The image factory built the pre-release for
the lab's schematics first, and no runner ran a CI job meanwhile.

| Step | v2026.10.18-3 | v2026.10.19 |
|---|---|---|
| The Controller's update by its updater | no answer for 0.41 s (the job: 13.8 s) | no answer for **0.41 s** (the job: 13.8 s) |
| The bundle for the lab's schematic | ready 48 s after the ask | ready **63 s** after |
| Rolling update under load: VIP | 0 lost / 2,330 | **1** lost (0.05 s) / 2,344, as the VIP went back to node 01 |
| Same: anycast | 1 lost (0.05 s) / 2,320 | **1** lost (0.05 s) / 2,334, as node 01 came back and took the route |
| Same: each node, from Upgrade to back in service | 14.1 s and 14.2 s | **13.2 s and 13.3 s** |
| Same: `wrk` | 12,887 req/s, 99th percentile 9.04 ms | 12,943 req/s, 99th percentile 11.52 ms |
| The longterm machine, updated through the Controller | ready 74 s after the call | ready **74 s** after; 6.18.57, 0 AVC denials, CIS 33/33 |

- **The VIP's lost request** came 0.7 s after node 01 was undrained,
  when its VRRP priority took the VIP back: one request, as in
  v2026.10.17-2's campaign (1 / 2,343), not the update itself.
- **`wrk`'s 16 read errors** are its 8 keep-alive connections, closed
  once by each node that reboots, as in every campaign.
- **The binaries moved again**: HAProxy, keepalived and BIRD took a new
  address on both nodes after the update.

### The Controller's new pages

Headless Chromium (the docs' pinned one) signed in to the lab's
Controller as its admin, 1440 × 900, with motion on: for each of the 18
palettes, light and dark, the node list as cards and as rows, then node
01's Overview, Metrics, Traffic flow and Storage - 216 pages, each left
6 to 12 seconds to sample, then axe (WCAG 2 A and AA, serious and
critical), page and console errors, failed requests.

| Run | Pages | Result |
|---|---|---|
| v2026.10.19, as published | 216 | axe `color-contrast` on **7**: Dracula light, Everforest dark, Flexoki dark, Nightfox dark, Nord dark, Rosé Pine light, Solarized dark - each time the "VM running" pill on the strips of the three machines the Controller made; one console error, below |
| The same, with #168's stylesheet laid over each page | 216 | **no problem** |

The console error, `net::ERR_NETWORK_CHANGED` on one Overview, most
likely came from the browser's machine itself: a build there was
starting Docker containers that minute, each bringing a network
interface up, and Chromium aborts a request in flight when the
machine's interfaces change. The second run had none.

**The pills.** Every status pill is a tint over what it sits on: on a
card that's `--surface`, the pair the palettes' test holds to AA. A
machine's strip on the node list is `--surface-2`, and the pill takes
its shade - lighter in dark mode, darker in light mode, nearer its text
either way. The test never composed that pair, and
the demo the pages were built on has no hypervisor machine, so its own
walk never met the strip. Worked out for the 36 palettes and modes, a
status pill on `--surface-2` fell under 4.5:1 for at least one status
in 18 of them, and further on `--surface-3`. [#168](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/168)
lays each pill's tint over `--surface` wherever it sits, which is the
pair the test holds, and adds a test that keeps it so: on the lab's pages, the 7 failures
were gone.

**The titles.** A card's title is set in small capitals, except one
that names something. The Controller's update notices - "The Controller
was updated to v2026.10.19" - weren't excepted, nor three cards titled
by a name (a module's daemon, its configuration file, a firewall set).
Fixed in the same PR.

**Not a defect**: on Metrics, the only page taken as a full-page
screenshot, the sidebar showed open. Checked apart: it is closed before
the screenshot, with no pointer over it and no focus in it, and open
only after - the full-page capture emulates a screen without a pointer
that hovers, and on such a screen the sidebar is meant to stay open.

**The fleet's curves.** With the node list open, `GET /api/nodes/metrics`
gave each of the seven nodes 120 readings - ten minutes, every 5 s -
of CPU, requests a second and traffic; one sampler for every page that
looks.

**What an open page costs a node.** Node 01's janusd, its CPU time read
from `/proc` through janusctl over 60 s, idle otherwise:

| Pages open | janusd |
|---|---|
| None | 0.07 % of a CPU |
| Metrics, a sample a second (the new default) | 0.28 % |
| Metrics, a sample every 5 s (the old default) | 0.12 % |
| The node list (the Controller samples each node every 5 s) | 0.10 % |
