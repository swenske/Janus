# v2026.10.26 pre-release tests

Tested in the afternoon of 2026-10-26. It is
[v2026.10.25-5](v2026.10.25-5.md) with:

- **a cluster's members say so**: a member's page names its cluster
  and its VRRP role, what the cluster manages is no longer offered for
  editing there, an admin may release the node's lock; the Nodes page
  frames a cluster's members together
  ([#334](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/334));
- **shipped logs name their cluster**: Elasticsearch's
  `orchestrator.cluster.*` and `janus.cluster.member`, RFC 5424's
  `[janus@32473 …]` ([#333](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/333));
- **the Controller's cluster metrics**, nine alerts and a Grafana
  dashboard ([#335](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/335));
- **Settings** for each account, refresh floors for an admin, the
  hypervisors and machines as lists
  ([#336](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/336));
- **the Traffic flow's rates** over the same 10 s for HAProxy and the
  interfaces, a TCP frontend without `option contstats` marked, dots
  that never restart ([#337](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/337)).

The same lab as the campaigns before; its HAProxy and kernel unchanged.

## Verdict

**Ready to promote.** Everything it adds works on the lab, the baseline
holds. One small defect, for the API only, has its fix
([#339](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/339)); it can ship with
the next release.

- **A VRRP cluster's members** (#334): the badge names the cluster and
  links its page, says "VRRP master" or "VRRP backup" and follows a
  failover; the managed pages say why nothing there is offered, and the
  node refuses a direct apply anyway - relayed as a 502 instead of the
  400 a refused file gets ([finding 1](#findings), #339). An admin's
  release from the page made the change possible, the cluster reported
  the drift on its page and in the metrics, and the next apply took it
  back.
- **The metrics and the alerts** (#335), scraped by a Prometheus with
  the repository's rules: a released member's drift fired its alert, a
  member's HAProxy stopped fired **JanusClusterPeerDown** after its 2
  minutes, and nothing else fired - one VRRP master all along.
- **Logs** (#333): the members' records in Elasticsearch carry the
  cluster; rsyslog parsed their RFC 5424 structured data. The lab's own
  Logstash was out of reach from the lab this afternoon - the network,
  not Janus ([finding 3](#findings)) - so a Debian rsyslog stood in.
- **Settings** (#336), in a browser: each choice followed by the pages -
  the refresh, an admin's floor, no metrics asked with the figures off,
  bits, UTC on a node's page; the lab's two hypervisors and their
  machines as lists.
- **The Traffic flow** (#337): without `option contstats`, a long
  download showed 0 B/s on its frontend and the hourglass; with it, the
  download's rate, the interface twice that - but one sample in five
  reads half ([finding 2](#findings)). The dots weren't made again at
  the page's refreshes.
- **The baseline**: the rolling update under load lost **nothing** on
  the VIP; HAProxy's throughput level with v2026.10.25's; zero AVC
  denials, the CIS audit 33/33.

## What this pre-release changes

### A cluster's members on the pages (#334)

keepalived among their extensions, made through the **New cluster** form
in 90 s; then a version with a virtual IP on the management network
(each member's priority a template variable, 150 and 100, HAProxy's
health weighing -100) and a frontend with a stick table on the cluster's
peers, the cluster managing HAProxy, its files and VRRP. The member
pages and the Nodes page read in a browser, as the lab's admin.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| A member's top bar | "vr47 · vr47-1" and **VRRP master**; the other "VRRP backup"; the badge links `/#/clusters/<id>` - followed, the cluster's page |
| HAProxy's configuration, files, certificates, VRRP | each says "… is managed by cluster vr47: change it on the cluster, every member alike - this node refuses a change made here", links the cluster and offers an admin **Release this node's lock…**; no **Apply…**, no **Apply this version…** in the history, no upload - validating and downloading stay |
| A configuration applied through a member's page API anyway | refused by the node: "HAProxy's configuration is managed by cluster vr47: change it on the cluster, or release this node's lock first (janusctl cluster release haproxy) - the cluster then reports this node as drifted" - **relayed as a 502** ([finding 1](#findings)); a file through the same API: 400 with the node's words |
| **Release this node's lock…** on HAProxy's configuration | its dialog says what follows; released (200), the page then offers **Apply…** and the history's versions |

|---|---|

## Findings

1. **A refused configuration came back as a 502** through the
   Controller's relay - "ApplyConfig stream: rpc error: code =
   FailedPrecondition desc = …" - where a refused file is a 400 with the
   node's words. A 502 says the node is out of reach. The pages don't
   offer the call on a member; an automation would retry.
   [#339](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/339) answers it as the
   unary calls' refusals.
2. **The Traffic flow's rate of a frontend with `option contstats`
   reads half one sample in five**: HAProxy adds a long stream's bytes
   every 5 s, and a 10 s window between samples holds one of those steps
   now and then instead of two. Its test accepts it ("give or take a
   step", 0.5 to 1.2 times the rate, the mean right). The interfaces'
   rate stays steady beside it. A rate measured from step to step would
   remove the dip - a design choice, not filed.
3. **The lab's Logstash was out of reach from the lab**: every lab host
   got "network is unreachable" for its TLS port, while its host
   answered on 443 and the port answered from outside the lab; at
   v2026.10.25-5's campaign that morning the nodes sent to it. The
   network's - nothing changed on the lab's router since then. A Debian
   rsyslog stood in for the structured data.
4. **`qemu-acme-ordered-test` flaked**: one "connection reset by peer"
   on its HTTP-01 fetch in the first attempt; it passed on the rerun and
   on the build box.
5. **A member whose HAProxy doesn't answer** keeps its last
   `janus_cluster_member_peers_up` and `_tables_synced` in the
   Controller's metrics while `janus_cluster_member_error` says it can't
   read them - the alerts that read the nodes' own exporters
   (JanusClusterPeerDown) aren't fooled. Noted, not filed.
