# v2026.11.04 pre-release tests

Tested on 2026-11-03, the hour it came out, on the lab where
v2026.11.03 was measured the same morning
([v2026.11.03's report](v2026.11.03.md)). What this pre-release
changes:

- **the fleet's trust, checked right**
  ([#518](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/518)): a node admitted
  less than two minutes ago that refuses the fleet's certificate is
  joining, tried again 15 s later, no longer "needs the recovery kit";
  a refused certificate whose TLS alert is lost is recognised as such;
- **USB devices' nodes under SELinux**
  ([#519](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/519)): the kernel's
  devtmpfs thread may make and remove `/dev/bus/usb/…`;
- **what a standby leaves behind**
  ([#482](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/482)): a stale
  `ha/control.sock` removed at start, the updater's copy leaving sockets
  out, `ha/previous/` kept to its two newest set-asides;
- **a refused update of an uploaded root filesystem says the slot holds
  the upload** ([#514](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/514)).

A short campaign: the Controller's update and its standby leftovers,
new machines and a manual admission followed every 0.5 s, USB devices
under SELinux against v2026.11.03, the upload's refusal, then the
baseline - the cluster updated in place under load, rollbacks,
HAProxy's throughput against v2026.11.03, the nodes' health.

## Verdict

**Everything this pre-release brings works on the lab, and the
baseline holds. My opinion: ready to promote.** The decision is yours.

- **The fleet's trust** (#518): two machines the Controller made from
  the pre-release's image, one on each host, ready in **14 and 17 s**;
  a node made by hand and approved by hand, then the same with
  v2026.11.03's image. `GET /api/fleet` every 0.5 s from the creation:
  **never `needs-kit`**; the joining node tried again **15.0 s** after
  its admission and trusted then. One cosmetic point: while joining,
  such a node is reported `trusted` - the page's "fleet" badge, not
  "joining the fleet…" ([finding 1](#findings)).
- **USB under SELinux** (#519): a USB tablet added to a node's machine
  and a USB keyboard plugged in and taken out live: **no AVC denial**
  on v2026.11.04, "USB disconnect" on the console; **v2026.11.03 logs
  the denial** (`kdevtmpfs` creating a `chr_file`) for the same plug.
- **The standby's leftovers** (#482): after the update, `ha/previous/`
  holds **two** set-asides (five before); a stale socket put in
  `ha/control.sock` is gone once the Controller restarts.
- **The upload's refusal** (#514): a damaged `rootfs.squashfs` uploaded,
  then the update from the staging: "**Slot B holds that upload, not
  its previous release**: Rollback won't switch to it - upload a sound
  bundle, then update" (v2026.11.03: "0 MiB written"); a mixed upload
  the same (v2026.11.03: "nothing was written"); Rollback refused both
  times; a sound upload repaired the node.
- The cluster updated in place onto the pre-release in **35.0 s, no
  probe lost**; rollbacks in 11.4 and 12.3 s; HAProxy's throughput as
  v2026.11.03's (the kernel and HAProxy are the same bytes); zero AVC
  denials, no OOM kill, no oops, enforcing, CIS 33/33 on seven nodes.

## What this pre-release changes

### The Controller's update and a standby's leftovers

The lab's Controller had been a standby in an earlier campaign. Before
the update: no `ha/control.sock`, **five** set-asides in
`ha/previous/`. Updated by its updater (v2026.10.07-3) from v2026.11.03,
the pre-release offered apart and installed with `"prerelease": true`:

| | v2026.11.03 (this morning) | v2026.11.04 |
|---|---|---|
| The updater's job | 30 s | 33.6 s, "Updated to v2026.11.04" |
| Without an answer | 12.25 s (120 of 610 probes) | **12.16 s** (119 of 612), none slower than 0.5 s |
| Its backup | 9 s | 10 s |
| `ha/previous/` afterwards | 5 set-asides | **2** - the two newest |

The update itself is no faster: the backup runs while the old
Controller is stopped, so it still copied the five set-asides; the new
Controller pruned them once it started. The next update copies two.

A stale socket - a Unix socket nothing listens on, owned by the
Controller's user, put at `ha/control.sock` - was **gone 5 s after the
Controller restarted**, without a log line. The lab's updater stays
v2026.10.07-3 (an update recreates the Controller's container only), so
its copy leaving sockets out isn't something the lab can show; CI's
`TestCopyAndReplace` covers it. The image's digest is
`controller-image.txt`'s; the certificate served unchanged.

### The fleet's trust for a new node

`GET /api/fleet` read every 0.5 s from the moment of each creation; a
line for each change of a new node's state.

| Node | Admitted | What `/api/fleet` said |
|---|---|---|
| A machine the Controller made, first host (its registration token) | 10 s after the call, ready at 14 s | `trusted` with the error "just admitted: the node takes the fleet's trust from its registration's answer - tried again in a few seconds" for 1.5 s, then `trusted` |
| A machine the Controller made, second host | 12 s after, ready at 17 s | `trusted` at once |
| A node made by hand from v2026.11.04's image, no token, approved by hand 0.03 s after it was listed | announced 12 s after its creation | `trusted`, then `trusted` with "just admitted…" for **15.0 s**, then `trusted` |
| The same with v2026.11.03's image | announced 9 s after | the same, 15.5 s |

**Never `needs-kit`**, and the joining node was tried again 15 s after
its admission, as #518 says; the node took the fleet's trust at its
next poll. The machines' images: the release's `janus-kvm.qcow2`, its
SHA-256 the asset's digest; 52 MiB checked by the Controller.

What the page shows meanwhile isn't "joining the fleet…": the
Controller's status turns a waiting node it knows to trust the fleet
(a keyless node, from its admission) into `trusted`, and the page
shows "fleet" - see [finding 1](#findings).

### USB devices under SELinux

On two nodes made by hand on the same host, one from each release's
image: stopped, a USB tablet (`<input type='tablet' bus='usb'/>`)
added to the machine's inactive definition on the `qemu-xhci`
controller it already had, started; then a USB keyboard plugged in
live, taken out 4 s later.

| | v2026.11.03 | v2026.11.04 |
|---|---|---|
| Boot with the tablet | no denial | no denial |
| A keyboard plugged in, then out | **1 denial**: `avc: denied { create } comm="kdevtmpfs" name="003" scontext=system_u:system_r:init_t tcontext=system_u:object_r:device_t tclass=chr_file` | **no denial** |
| The console | "usb 1-2: USB disconnect, device number 3" | the same |

At boot the tablet is enumerated 1.6 s after the kernel starts, before
init loads the policy (1.9 s): on the lab's hosts the boot race Proxmox
loses doesn't happen, for either release; the live plug, well after
the policy, shows the denial and its fix. A tablet on Proxmox itself
wasn't tested here.

### The upload's refusal

On a node of the pre-release (its default image, 128 MiB slots, slot A
running), bundles from the release's own assets, their digests
checked:

| Upload | The update from the staging | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Damaged**: one byte of `rootfs.squashfs` changed at 2 MiB, its `.sha256` made to match | refused 0.2 s after the call: "rootfs.squashfs: the data isn't what the table describes: block 512 (byte 2097152) isn't the one the hash tree holds - it isn't the one uki-b.efi was built with (damaged on its way, or the bundle mixes two builds), and the node couldn't mount its root from it. **Slot B holds that upload, not its previous release**: Rollback won't switch to it - upload a sound bundle, then update" | `version`: "Other slot: B - holds an uploaded root filesystem its update refused"; **Rollback refused** ("slot B doesn't hold what its kernel image boots ... update it again instead"); HAProxy answering |
| **Mixed**: v2026.11.04's kernel images and `rootfs.verity`, v2026.11.03's `rootfs.squashfs` (the same size) | refused at "block 0 (byte 0)", then the same "Slot B holds that upload, not its previous release" | Rollback refused |
| The release's sound bundle | checked ("the release this node runs"), slot B written, rebooted 0.8 s after the call | v2026.11.04 on slot B, slot A v2026.11.04 |

v2026.11.03 said "(0 MiB written)" and "nothing was written" for the
same two uploads (its report's finding 2).

## Findings

1. **A joining node is reported `trusted`.** While a keyless node takes
   the fleet's trust (up to 15 s after a manual approval), the trust
   loop records it as waiting with the error "just admitted: …", but
   `GET /api/fleet` turns a waiting node the store knows to trust the
   fleet into `trusted` - so the API says `trusted` with that error,
   and the page shows the green "fleet" badge, not "joining the fleet…"
   as the release notes say. The alarm #518 removes is gone, and the
   node does trust the fleet 15 s later; only what the page says in
   between is early. Cosmetic: the status could keep `waiting` while
   the tracker holds a joining error, or the notes could say the badge
   reads "fleet".

Also noticed, not this release's: a hypervisor's ACPI shutdown
(`virsh shutdown`) does nothing on a node in 60 s - the Controller
stops its machines through the node's API, so only a stop from the
hypervisor itself is affected.

  and HAProxy.
