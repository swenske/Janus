# v2026.10.24-4 pre-release tests

Tested on the morning of 2026-10-25. It is
[v2026.10.24-3](v2026.10.24-3.md) with what
[its full campaign](v2026.10.24-3.md) found:

- a restarted janusd stops its extensions' daemons first, and they stop
  if janusd ever dies - no orphan left beside the next janusd's own
  ([#280](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/280));
- "in sync" on a busy host: a peer behind at a first read is read again
  every 200 ms for up to 2 s
  ([#277](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/277));
- the node pages' last WCAG 2 A findings
  ([#279](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/279));
- `janusctl system sysctl cancel` prints the values going back, and
  `system audit`'s help says it follows the log live
  ([#278](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/278)).

The same kernel and HAProxy as v2026.10.24-3. The baseline compared with
[v2026.10.24-3](v2026.10.24-3.md)'s figures, then what this
pre-release changes, on the lab's real nodes and Controller, and a
quick pass on the clusters - #280 touches the daemons their VRRP runs
on. The rest of the features had their full campaign on v2026.10.24-3
and are unchanged since.

## Verdict

**Not ready as is**, for two findings that come with their fixes:

1. **A janusd restart, and a shutdown by the hypervisor through the
   guest agent, drop the virtual IP before the peer has it**
   ([finding 1](#findings)): 3 requests lost under load on the VRRP
   master's restart, 2 on its shutdown by the hypervisor, and a node
   alone without its virtual IP for 4.9 s while it restarted. #280 made a
   restart stop keepalived with janusd, which is right, but without the
   drain a reboot already does; the hypervisor's shutdown always went
   this way. Fixed and proven on a real boot
   ([#285](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/285)).
2. **`janusctl system restart -h` restarted janusd**, and `system
   shutdown -h` would have powered the node off ([finding 2](#findings)).
   Fixed: a command's `-h` shows its help and runs nothing
   ([#284](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/284)).

Everything else does what it says. Nothing regressed: the Controller's
update, the image factory, the rolling update under load (nothing lost
on the VIP at the swaps), the failovers, the reboots and the throughput
are level with v2026.10.24-3's. This pre-release's fixes hold on real
nodes: **a restarted janusd brings each extension daemon back exactly
once** (no orphan, no "already running", zero AVC denials); the members
**in sync at every sample under the three runners' load** (46,767 req/s,
every request a stick-table update); **the six node pages pass axe**
in both modes; the cancelled trial printed the right way. The
cluster's VRRP master recycled under load lost nothing on the VIP, a
third time.

A next pre-release with #285 and #284, its lab campaign measuring the
restart and the hypervisor's shutdown again.

## What this pre-release changes

### A janusd restart with the extensions (#280)

`janusd` restarted three ways, each time with the extension daemons
listed before and after (`janusctl system ps`), the services' states,
janusd's lines about its extensions, the AVC denials - and the probes
every 50 ms on the addresses the node serves.

| Where | How | Probes | The daemons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A machine with every extension, a VRRP instance of its own (no peer) | `janusctl system restart` | its own address (HAProxy): nothing lost but one stray probe 30 s later; **its virtual IP: 96 lost, 4.94 s** | before and after: one janusd, one HAProxy, one BIRD, one Consul, one node_exporter, one guest agent, keepalived's two (itself and its VRRP child); every service healthy; 0 AVC |
| Node 01, the VRRP master, under `wrk` (12,455 req/s) | `janusctl system restart` | **VIP: 3 lost** (0.15 s at the swap to node 02), node 02 holding it 2.9 s; anycast: nothing lost | one of each again, healthy, 0 AVC |
| Node 02, the backup, under `wrk` | the node page's **Restart janusd** | nothing lost | one of each again, healthy, 0 AVC |

The orphans of [v2026.10.24-3](v2026.10.24-3.md#findings) are gone:
every daemon came back exactly once, and no "already running", bind
failure or unhealthy restart loop was logged. What a restart costs now
is the virtual IP. On the alone machine, Restart replied, waited its 2 s
reply grace, then stopped every extension at once: keepalived removed
the IP at that instant, but janusd only exited once Consul had left its
cluster (about 3 s, the lab having no Consul server). The next janusd
started keepalived, which took the IP back 0.8 s later. On the VRRP
master, keepalived gave the IP up before node 02 had it. Before #280
the orphaned keepalived kept the IP through a restart, unmanaged:
nothing was lost, but nothing supervised it either. See
[finding 1](#findings).

A janusd killed hard (Pdeathsig) could not be tried on the lab: there is
no shell, the guest agent's `guest-exec` is disabled, and janusd is the
last process the OOM killer would pick.

**A shutdown by the hypervisor** through the guest agent (Proxmox's
Shutdown button does the same), measured too because it goes through
the same stop: node 01 under `wrk` (9,903 req/s) was off 4.0 s after
the call and started again; **VIP: 2 lost** (0.11 s at the swap), the
anycast address nothing lost at the shutdown (one at the swap back).
janusd's SIGTERM path stops the extensions before HAProxy, whose drain
comes too late to help.

### In sync under the heaviest load (#277)

The lab's two nodes in a cluster with a stick table tracking every
request, the three runners loading the VIP at once (46,767 req/s, 90 s),
each member's `janusctl cluster status` and exporter read every 5 s:
**22 samples on each member, in sync at every one** - status 1/1,
`janus_cluster_peer_table_synced` 1. A scrape took 57 to 78 ms under
that load (4 ms idle), the status 0.04 to 0.12 s: no 200 ms re-read
showed - the peers kept up at the first read.

### The node pages (#279), `sysctl cancel` (#278)

**Every page** - the Controller's 9 tabs, the 36 pages of a node with
every extension and the Let's Encrypt app, in two palettes and both
modes, headless Chromium: 184 pages, no page error, no console error,
no failed request, and **axe on WCAG 2 A/AA: nothing** (v2026.10.24-3:
six findings in each mode, on Service logs, Files, Kernel, Events,
Consul and Sysctl).

**A cancelled trial**: a kernel parameter put on trial without
confirming it (60 → 45), then cancelled: `janusctl` printed `45 → 60`
under "the values from before the trial are back", and the node said
60. `janusctl help system audit` says "live".

## Findings

1. **A janusd restart and a shutdown by the hypervisor drop the virtual
   IP before the peer has it** (above): 3 requests lost on the VRRP
   master's restart under load, 2 on its shutdown through the guest
   agent, a node alone without its virtual IP for 4.9 s. A reboot
   already drains first - HAProxy reads unhealthy to keepalived and
   BIRD, which let the traffic go, for `-haproxy-drain` (2 s) - and a
   negative track weight (the lab's -100) has the peer preempt with no
   gap. Restart and janusd's SIGTERM path stopped keepalived instead,
   with Consul's leave holding the exit on top.
   [#285](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/285): Restart drains
   first, the SIGTERM path stops HAProxy (and its drain) first, and
   keepalived and BIRD stop last of the extensions. `qemu-vrrp-test`
   (two enforcing keepalived nodes, now with the guest agent) restarts
   janusd on the master and shuts it down through the guest agent: with
   the fix the master leaves MASTER before its keepalived stops, the
   backup taking the IP; the released janusd fails both checks. The
   losses themselves are for the next pre-release's lab campaign: the
   lab runs signed release images only.
2. **`janusctl system restart -h` restarted janusd.** Typed for the
   command's help. `system restart` and `system shutdown` take no flag
   and read no argument: `system shutdown -h` would have powered the
   node off. Commands with flags printed their usage, by luck of Go's
   flag parsing. [#284](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/284):
   a command given `-h`, `-help` or `--help` prints its help and runs
   nothing; with the branch's `janusctl` on the lab, `system restart -h`
   and `system shutdown --help` printed their help, janusd's process
   unchanged.
3. **The old member's drain journaled twice** in a recycle - cosmetic,
   as in the two previous campaigns.
