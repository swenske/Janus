Tested late on the afternoon of 2026-10-27. It is
[v2026.10.27-2](v2026.10.27-2.md) with one change,
[#363](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/363), in the docs only: the
Claude Code page says how to keep the user's other credentials out of

## Verdict

**Ready to promote.** The update path and what v2026.10.27-2 brought
still hold on the new build:

- **The rolling update under load**: one probe lost on the VIP and one
  on the anycast address, each alone, a minute and more after the last
  node was back in service ([finding 1](#findings)) - nothing lost while
  the nodes were updated.
- **`janusctl haproxy server-state` and `haproxy reload`** with janusctl
  v2026.10.27-3 on the node holding the VIP: **0 lost** of 980 VIP and
  971 anycast probes; the drained server got no request.
- Zero AVC denials, no OOM kill, the CIS audit 33/33 on both nodes.

## Findings

1. **Two probes lost alone, after the rolling update** - one on the VIP
   (64 s after the last node came back in service) and one on the
   anycast address (103 s after): each a single request without an
   answer in 1 s, between answers from the same node, while nothing
   changed on the lab. The path from the runner through the lab's
   router, not the nodes - the anycast probe v2026.10.27-2's campaign
   lost was the same kind. Nothing to fix in this release.
2. **`qemu-acme-*-test`'s HTTP-01 fetch flakes** - here
   `qemu-acme-ordered-test` again ("acme:error:connection" fetching the
   challenge), after two builds without it - it had failed four builds
   in a row before them; passed on the rerun.
   Worth finding out why, as
   [v2026.10.26-2's report](v2026.10.26-2.md#findings) already says.
3. **The screenshots job caught the splash over the page** - on both
   attempts, one shot each: `node-update-dark` (3.7 % of its pixels) on
   the first, `node-update-light` (3.6 %) on the second. The drift
   artifact shows the page as committed with the Controller's splash -
   the logo and its bar - drawn over it, although the harness waits for
   `.splash` to be detached before shooting (`hack/browser/shots.mjs`):
   the splash came back after, or the wait didn't see it. Not a change
   of the page (the other theme, shot a second apart, matched), and not
   the GitHub pre-releases (the screenshots' Controller reads a local
   release list); v2026.10.27-2's build passed this job. The harness
   to look at; it doesn't gate a release.
4. **The lab's syslog target is still blocked on the network's side** -
   as in [v2026.10.27-2's campaign](v2026.10.27-2.md#findings); not
   Janus.
