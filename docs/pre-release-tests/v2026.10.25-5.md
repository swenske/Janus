# v2026.10.25-5 pre-release tests

Tested in the morning of 2026-10-26, a short campaign on what
[v2026.10.25-3's](v2026.10.25-3.md) and [v2026.10.25-4's](v2026.10.25-4.md)
campaigns found. It is v2026.10.25-4 with:

- **the Controller's libvirt machines without a variable store on a
  host prepared as the docs say**: libvirt's choice of firmware read
  back and made stateless before the first start
  ([#326](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/326));
- **Let's Encrypt changing hands in one check**, without re-ordering,
  the members told one at a time
  ([#325](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/325));
- **the audit keeps a request's query**
  ([#328](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/328));
- the clusters guide: from a node, RouterOS
  ([#324](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/324)).

The same lab as the two campaigns before.

## Verdict

**Ready to promote.** Each of v2026.10.25-3's and -4's findings holds
fixed on the lab:

- **No variable store** (#326): two new machines, one on each host,
  defined with the plain OVMF code alone, `stateless='yes'`, no
  `<nvram>`, and no variable store file on either host - the hosts'
  polkit rules as the guide writes them, read as the Controller's own
  account. Ten firmware reboots of one: 4.9 to 5.1 s.
- **Let's Encrypt changing hands** (#325), on the page: **one check**
  handed over - nothing ordered again, the old orderer answering
  HTTP-01 with the new one's account right after, a renewal validated
  through it, no certificate "due", and the stick tables kept (400 keys
  on each member before and after).
- **The audit** (#328) shows `?machines=destroy` on the cluster's
  deletion with its machines - which went from both hosts in 6 s - and
  `?force=true`.
- **The baseline smoke check**: the rolling update under load lost
  **nothing** on the VIP nor on the anycast route; zero AVC denials, the
  CIS audit 33/33.

## What this pre-release changes

### No variable store (#326)

A cluster of two new machines, anti-affinity required, the letsencrypt,
node_exporter and QEMU agent extensions, made through the **New
cluster** form in 88 s (the pre-release's base image fetched to both
hosts). Their definitions read on each host as the Controller's account,
under the polkit rules of [hypervisors](../hypervisors.md):

| Read | v2026.10.25-3 and -4 | v2026.10.25-5 |
|---|---|---|
| The `<os>` of both machines | `firmware='efi'`, `<loader … format='raw'>OVMF_CODE_4M.fd`, `<nvram template='…OVMF_VARS_4M.fd'>` | `<loader readonly='yes' type='pflash' stateless='yes' format='raw'>/usr/share/OVMF/OVMF_CODE_4M.fd</loader>`, **no `<nvram>`** |
| Variable store files on the hosts | one per machine | **none** - the hosts' files, before and after: the machines' domains, disks, NoCloud volumes and the base image only |
| Firmware reboots | - | ten through the node's page: **4.9 to 5.1 s** (median 5.0); the kernel's "Restarting system" to the firmware's boot manager 0.70 s each |

v2026.10.25-3's campaign measured the difference: 45 reboots each, 5.7
s at most without a store, one at 41.1 s with one.

### Let's Encrypt changing hands (#325)

The cluster above, its secrets from its first member. The lab's private
ACME CA (Pebble) through the lab's proxy, its DNS answering every name
with the **second** member's address. A version with the HTTP-01 rule
and a stick table replicated over the cluster's peers (a string key from
the query, `http_req_cnt`), then one with a TLS frontend; 400 keys sent.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| Let's Encrypt on the first member, a check | "ordered by le46-1", the second pulled its configuration; both certificates valid 3 s after a renewal asked, validated on the second; the next check pulled them |
| The second member, following | both certificates **valid** (v2026.10.25-3: "due"); its HTTP-01 answer the first's account |
| **Secrets come from** set to the second on the page, **Check now** | the change at :07.66, the check done at :09.77; journaled in **that check** "ordered by le46-2" - the second told alone, then both (their logs) |
| Right after | the CA issued **nothing** (2 certificates before, 2 after - v2026.10.25-3: both ordered again); **both members answer HTTP-01 with the second's account** (v2026.10.25-3: the first its own until the next check); both certificates valid on both; the first "Ordered by le46-2" |
| The stick table | **400 keys on each member before and after**; both HAProxy restarted at the handover, one second apart |
| The CA pointed at the **first** member (its DNS changed, the CA not restarted), a renewal asked of the second | **validated through the first, issued** (v2026.10.25-3: 403); the next check: "le46-1 pulled le46-2's renewed certificate(s): site"; both serve it, verified against the CA's chain; the tables still 400 and 400 |
