# v2026.10.29-5 pre-release tests

Tested in the evening of 2026-10-29: a very short pass on the one fix
since [v2026.10.29-4](v2026.10.29-4.md), whose lab pass found it.
It is v2026.10.29-4 with
[#415](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/415): init no longer stops
supervising janusd once an update's deadline has passed - a node booted
from an update whose janusd exits (a Restart, a crash) gets it back -;
and the guide's Prometheus `filter` corrected. Everything else is in the
reports of [v2026.10.29-4](v2026.10.29-4.md) and the campaigns before.

## Verdict

**Ready to promote.** On a machine the Controller updated, janusd
restarted long after the update's confirmation comes back in about a
second, with its extensions - three times, the last ten minutes later.
v2026.10.29-4, on the same machine, left janusd down until a power
reset. The revert path stays covered by CI. Nothing new was found.

One machine the Controller made on v2026.10.29-4 with BIRD, Consul,

runs. Consul, without a configuration, waits.

### On v2026.10.29-4, the defect

The machine's extensions changed through the Controller - an update on
v2026.10.29-4, with `wait_for_health`, confirmed:

| `janusctl system restart` | Result |
|---|---|
| 29 s after that boot | janusd back 2.2 s after the call - within init's deadline |
| 6 minutes after that boot | janusd gone 4.1 s after the call and **never back**: the console's last line `init: /sbin/janusd (pid 106) exited (0)`, BIRD and node_exporter stopped with it, the Controller's node page 502 - until a power reset |

### On v2026.10.29-5, the fix

The same machine updated by the Controller to v2026.10.29-5 (`PATCH
/api/machines/{id}` with the version, the guest agent back in its
extensions): the image factory built that schematic in 62 s, the machine
was ready on the new slot 75.5 s after the call, "bootcommit: confirmed
healthy for slot A" a second after its boot. Then:

| `janusctl system restart` | janusd back | Console | Its services |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.5 minutes after the update's boot | 5.3 s after the call (1.2 s after the old one left) | `init: /sbin/janusd (pid 85) exited (0)`, **`init: this boot is confirmed - janusd is restarted as on any boot`**, `janusd v2026.10.29-5 listening` | BIRD, node_exporter and the guest agent started again, Consul waiting as before; the Controller's node page 200 |
| 2 minutes later | 5.3 s after the call | `exited (0)`, then janusd at once - the deadline dropped the first time | the same |
| 10 minutes later | 5.2 s after the call | `exited (0)`, then janusd at once | the same |

**The node's health** on v2026.10.29-5: SELinux enforcing, zero AVC denials
(this boot and the one before), no OOM kill, no oops, the CIS audit
33/33. The Controller's log since its update: no panic, no error outside
the audit.
