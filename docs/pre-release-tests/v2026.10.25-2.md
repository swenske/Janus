# v2026.10.25-2 pre-release tests

Tested in the evening of 2026-10-25. It is
[v2026.10.25](v2026.10.25.md) with:

- what [its campaign](v2026.10.25.md) found: a recycle with
  anti-affinity *preferred* keeps the replaced member's host when the
  others would mean sharing one; a scale the hosts can't give is refused
  at once (400); a recycle journals the old member's drain once; the
  refusals say what the request's earlier machines took, and the bounds
  in seconds ([#303](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/303));
- **the balancer hook**: a balancer in front of a cluster told which
  members serve - before one is drained or removed, once one is in
  service - signed, from the Controller's page, its API, `janusctl` and
  Terraform ([#308](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/308));
- **the Controller's metrics**: `GET /api/metrics`, each cluster's
  members, load and auto-scaling
  ([#302](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/302));
- **node-to-node state transfer**: `janusctl -n NEW system state import
  -from OLD` ([#304](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/304));
- the docs on the slow firmware reboot
  ([#307](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/307)).

The same kernel and HAProxy as v2026.10.25, on the same lab: two KVM
hosts, the 32 GB one with plain SATA drives
([v2026.10.25's finding 4](v2026.10.25.md#findings)). The baseline
compares with v2026.10.25's figures, then what this pre-release
changes.

## Verdict

**Ready to promote**, with the guide's advice of
[#313](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/313) for a balancer in
front ([finding 1](#findings)).

- **v2026.10.25's regression is fixed** (#303): the same recycle - a
  member alone on the 16 GB host, anti-affinity preferred - put its
  replacement on that host, the cluster's page without a problem; the
  journal named the old member's drain once.
- **The balancer hook** (#308), with an HAProxy in front of a cluster
  whose servers only the hook set, under 25,000 requests a second:
  - a drain told the balancer first, then drained the member after the
    drain delay, and an undrain told it back;
  - a member more was told in once it served, a member less told out
    before its drain, then the list without it, its machine destroyed;
  - nothing was lost once the balancer resent a request whose answer
    came back empty - without that, a member's reload broke 5 to 11
    requests in 5 million ([finding 1](#findings));
  - a hook answering 503 refused the drain, the member serving on, until
    it answered again;
  - Terraform's `balancer` block was set, changed and removed in place.
- **The Controller's metrics** (#302) gave each cluster's members, load
  and auto-scaling to a reader's token - the load the mean of the
  members' own.
- **Node to node** (#304): a node's state pulled by a fresh one in one
  call, its identity only when asked, the grant refused to a reader and
  the import to a node in use.
- **The baseline holds**: the rolling update under load lost **nothing**
  on the VIP; failovers, kexec and the Controller's machines are level;
  zero AVC denials, the CIS audit 33/33.

Node 01's firmware reboot took **42.5 s** once: its UEFI firmware
compacting a full variable store, as #307 explains - seen in the lab
for the first time with the store measured right after
([finding 3](#findings)). The image build's screenshot check failed on
the cluster page, which now has the balancer card
([finding 2](#findings)).

### The balancer hook (#308)

A balancer in front of a two-member cluster of new machines: an HAProxy
3.2 in a container beside the lab's Controller, no health check of its
own, its servers set only through its runtime API by a receiver of the
hook - which checks each call's signature, applies the members in
service, then answers 204 (or 503, when told to). The hook set with a
secret and a 3 s drain delay. `wrk` (two threads, 16 keep-alive
connections) and a probe every 50 ms through the balancer, from the
machine that isn't a runner.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|

## Findings

1. **A balancer in front must resend a request whose answer came back
   empty.** Every member's HAProxy reloads when the cluster's members
   change - its peers section names them - and when a version is
   applied. Its old process closes its idle keep-alive connections, and
   a balancer that keeps connections to the members sends a request on
   one now and then at that very moment. Without retries, 5 to 11
   requests in about 5 million broke per scale, and 5 for a version
   applied; with `retry-on conn-failure empty-response
   response-timeout`, none. The hook itself did its part: the member was
   out of the balancer before it was drained. Not new - the reloads are
   HAProxy's -, but the balancer hook makes the case common.
   [#313](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/313) puts it in the
   guide, with the lines for an HAProxy in front.
2. **The cluster page's screenshot changed**: the image build's
   screenshot check (which only reports) found the cluster page, light
   and dark, 0.37 and 0.38 % different - the new **Balancer in front**
   card. The docs' screenshots want taking again (`make
   docs-screenshots`); the site shows the old ones until then.
3. **The slow firmware reboot, seen as #307 explains it.** Node 01's
   firmware reboot took 42.5 s: 35.6 s between the kernel's "machine
   restart" and the firmware's boot manager. Right after, its UEFI
   variable store was used up to 0.9 % of its 256 KiB - compacted on
   that boot; at v2026.10.25's campaign it was 71.5 % full, about 13
   boots from full. Node 02's store is at 42 %. No virtio-net line
   around it. The lab's two nodes are virtual machines made by hand,
   with a variable store; the Controller's machines are to run without
   one - a change made after this pre-release.

## Not tested

  fleet, and the lab's nodes trust its Controller's; the engine is the
  same (unit tests, `qemu-controller-cluster-test`,

- **Auto-scaling acting** behind the balancer: the members were set by
  hand; auto-scaling observed only.
- **A balancer hook over HTTPS with a CA given**: the receiver was plain
  HTTP beside the Controller (the unit tests use TLS with its own CA).
- **`janus_cluster` on Proxmox VE**, **a janusd killed hard**,
  **restoring a backup**, **arm64**, **IPv6**: as in
  [v2026.10.25](v2026.10.25.md#not-tested).
