# v2026.10.25-4 pre-release tests

Tested in the night of 2026-10-25, a short campaign right after
v2026.10.25-3's ([#327](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/327)), on the same lab. It is v2026.10.25-3
with:

- **deleting a cluster may destroy its members' virtual machines**
  (`DELETE /api/clusters/{id}?machines=destroy`, a box in the deletion's
  dialog) ([#322](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/322));
- **a Proxmox image storage that can't take uploads said before
  anything is made** ([#320](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/320));
- the cluster page's screenshot taken again
  ([#321](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/321)).

## Verdict

**Not ready as is** - for v2026.10.25-3's findings, which it carries:
the Controller's libvirt machines keep their variable store on a
prepared host ([#326](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/326)) - the
cluster made here had one again -, and Let's Encrypt changing hands
re-orders and answers HTTP-01 wrongly for a check
([#325](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/325)). See
v2026.10.25-3's report, its findings ([#327](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/327)).

What this pre-release adds works as described:

- **A cluster deleted with its machines**, from its page with the box
  ticked: the members left, then their virtual machines, disks and
  variable stores went from both hosts, within 12 s - nothing else
  touched. Deleted without the box, a cluster's machines stay, ready.
  The audit can't tell the two apart ([finding 1](#findings),
  [#328](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/328)).
- **The baseline smoke check**: the rolling update under load lost
  **nothing** on the VIP nor on the anycast route; zero AVC denials, the
  CIS audit 33/33.

## What this pre-release changes

### A cluster deleted with its machines (#322)

A cluster of two new machines, anti-affinity required, one on each host,
made through the **New cluster** form (30 s); both hosts' libvirt
domains, the Controller's pool's volumes and the variable stores listed
before, with it, and after.

| Step | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Delete** on the cluster's page | the dialog: "Delete the members' virtual machines too (del45-1, del45-2) - destroyed once they left, their disks with them", unticked; the cluster's name to type |
| Ticked, confirmed | `DELETE …?machines=destroy` 202; "Cluster del45 is being deleted, its members' virtual machines destroyed."; no console error |
| Within 12 s | the cluster gone, both machines gone from the Controller; on both hosts their domains, disks, NoCloud volumes and variable stores gone - **everything else identical**, the release's base image kept (as designed). The Controller's log: "cluster del45 deleted by admin, its 2 member machine(s) being destroyed" |
| The audit | `DELETE /api/clusters/<id>` 202 - **without its query**: a deletion keeping the machines reads the same ([finding 1](#findings)) |
| v2026.10.25-3's cluster deleted **without** the box | "Cluster seed44 is being deleted."; gone in 5 s, its two machines **ready, on both hosts unchanged**, each member "Cluster: none". Then destroyed by hand |

### The Proxmox image storage (#320) and the screenshots (#321)

## Findings

1. **The audit doesn't say a cluster's deletion destroyed its
   machines.** The entry records the path without its query: `DELETE
   /api/clusters/<id>?machines=destroy` and a deletion keeping the
   machines both read `DELETE /api/clusters/<id> 202`; the machines'
   records and the cluster's journal go with them, so only the
   Controller's log says who destroyed the machines. `?force=true` and
   `?forget=true` are lost the same way.
   [#328](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/328) records the query.
2. **v2026.10.25-3's findings stand**: the cluster's two machines had a
   variable store again (#326), and #325's Let's Encrypt handover isn't
   in this pre-release either.
