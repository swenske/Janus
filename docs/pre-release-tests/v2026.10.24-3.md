# v2026.10.24-3 pre-release tests

Tested on the night of 2026-10-24 to 25. It is
[v2026.10.24-2](v2026.10.24-2.md) with what
[its campaign](v2026.10.24-2.md) found:

- the member serving the traffic read "catching up" under load: a peer
  behind at a first read of `show peers` is read again half a second
  later ([#273](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/273));
- a node removed from its cluster stayed drained without saying so, and
  joined again drained: it now says so, and a node that joins is put
  back in service ([#274](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/274)).

The same kernel and HAProxy as v2026.10.24-2. A **full campaign**, at
the user's request: every scenario of [Lab test scenarios](lab-scenarios.md)
and every feature on the lab's real nodes and Controller, not only what
this pre-release changes. The baseline compared with
[v2026.10.24-2](v2026.10.24-2.md)'s figures; then the node's features,
the Controller's, and the clusters.

## Verdict

**Not ready as is**, for one defect in janusd that this pre-release
didn't introduce - [finding 1](#findings): **a restarted janusd leaves
its extensions' daemons running, orphaned, and the ones it starts again
fail for good** (fixed and proven on a real boot,
[#280](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/280)). Everything else does
what it says. Nothing regressed: the Controller's update, the image
factory, the rolling update under load (nothing lost on the VIP), the
failovers, the reboots and the throughput are level with
v2026.10.24-2's. Both fixes of this pre-release hold on real nodes:
the members **in sync under load** at every sample, and a member
removed **says it is drained** and **serves again when it joins**. The
cluster's VRRP master recycled under load with no request lost on the
VIP, again. Two smaller findings come with PRs: six WCAG 2 A findings
on node pages no check visited
([#279](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/279)), and `janusctl
system sysctl cancel` printing the cancelled trial the wrong way round
([#278](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/278)).

A next pre-release with #280, #279, #278 and
[#277](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/277) (the in-sync re-read
on a busy host, below).

## Findings

1. **A restarted janusd leaves its extensions' daemons running, and the
   ones it starts fail for good.** Seen from an AVC denial on the
   every-extension machine (`bird` writing to `bird.ctl`), after the
   operator test restarted janusd (`SystemService/Restart`, the node
   page's Restart, `janusctl system restart`): every daemon of the
   previous janusd - BIRD, keepalived, Consul, node_exporter, the QEMU
   guest agent - still ran, orphaned and unmanaged (its output going
   nowhere), and the new janusd started its own beside them:
   keepalived "daemon is already running", Consul unable to bind,
   node_exporter and the guest agent in a restart loop, a second BIRD
   connecting to the first's socket (the denial). Restart exits without
   stopping them, and nothing ties them to janusd. HAProxy is unaffected
   (the next janusd takes it over, by design). Not new in this
   pre-release: the extensions' supervision has always worked this way.
   [#280](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/280): Restart stops the
   extensions before it exits, and their daemons get SIGTERM if janusd
   ever dies (Pdeathsig); `qemu-extensions-test` now restarts janusd and
   checks each daemon is back once, healthy, HAProxy untouched - it
   fails on the released janusd and passes with the fix.
2. **Six WCAG 2 A findings on node pages no check visited**, in both
   modes: the Service logs page's "lines" select and the Files page's
   path field without a name, and four scrolling boxes the keyboard
   couldn't reach (the kernel's log, the events, the Consul resolvers,
   the kernel parameters' history).
   [#279](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/279) names them and
   makes the boxes focusable; `hack/ui-preview`'s check now walks those
   pages (and found a seventh, the Files listing).
3. **`janusctl system sysctl cancel` prints the trial, not what went
   back** ("30 → 60" under "the values from before the trial are
   back", where the node put 30 back); and `janusctl system audit`
   follows the log without its help saying so.
   [#278](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/278).
4. **On a busy host, "in sync" can still flap** - the first attempt of
   this pre-release's build. Not seen on the lab's nodes (in sync at
   every sample under load); [#277](https://github.com/swenske/Janus/pull/277).
5. **A 700 MB cache no longer fits beside the serving HAProxy on
   1 GiB** (above): the outcome is the designed one - the new process
   killed, the serving one kept, the configuration refused -, the
   auto-scaling scenario now measures it as an OOM kill.

  has none).
